Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

The short version

BlackjackTrack collects no data. Everything you enter stays on your device. We can't see it, and neither can anyone else.

Data collection

BlackjackTrack does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data. The app contains:

  • No user accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics or telemetry
  • No advertising or third-party SDKs
  • No network connections to our servers (we don't operate any)

Where your data lives

All session records, trip records, casino names, and settings are stored in a local database inside the app's private container on your device. If your device is backed up (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), the app's data is included in that backup under the terms of Apple's backup services — that process is controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

Exports

The app can export your data to CSV or PDF files at your request. Where those files go — email, cloud storage, printing — is entirely under your control through the iOS share sheet. We never receive a copy.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes all of its data from your device. There is nothing to delete on any server, because nothing ever leaves your device.

Children

BlackjackTrack is intended for users of legal gambling age and is rated accordingly on the App Store. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children — the app collects no information at all.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be noted in the app's release notes.
