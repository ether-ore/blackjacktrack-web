How EV Is Estimated

The same methodology page that ships inside the app — published here so you can judge the math before you pay for it.

The formula

Hourly EV = hands/hour × average bet × net edge

Net edge is your counting advantage minus the house edge for the table's rules. Session EV multiplies hourly EV by the hours you logged.

House edge — published values

The baseline is a 6-deck, S17, DAS, no-surrender, 3:2 game at 0.40% house edge. Rule adjustments use the published effects from the Wizard of Odds rule-variation tables:

  • Dealer hits soft 17 (H17): +0.22%
  • No double after split: +0.14%
  • Late surrender offered: −0.08%
  • Blackjack pays 6:5: +1.39%
  • Blackjack pays even money: +2.27%
  • Single deck (vs 6): −0.46% · Double deck (vs 6): −0.17% · 8 decks (vs 6): +0.02%

For a basic-strategy player who isn't counting, expected loss is simply the house edge times money through the table — this part of the math is accurate to a few hundredths of a percent.

Counting advantage — an estimate

When a counting system is selected, the model adds advantage that scales with the system's betting correlation and the size of your bet spread (logarithmically — going from 1-8 to 1-16 helps less than going from flat to 1-8). Further adjustments:

  • Penetration scales the edge relative to a 75% baseline — deeper is better
  • CSM sets counting advantage to zero — the count never develops
  • Wong in/out raises per-hand edge roughly 40% and halves hands played
  • Average bet assumes about 30% of hands are played at elevated counts

Hands per hour

Estimated from game type and table crowding: roughly 200 hands/hour heads-up at a shoe game down to ~55 at a full seven-player table. You can override with actual hands played on any session.

The variance band

The shaded band in the EV vs Actual report is ±1 standard deviation, scaled by the square root of hands played. Flat betting runs about 1.15 × your average bet per hand. Bet spreading adds variance beyond what the larger average bet captures: a 1-12 play-all spread runs closer to 1.34 × average bet (variance ≈ 1.80), per Don Schlesinger's Blackjack Attack figures. Roughly two out of three outcomes should land inside the band.

Version note: app version 1.0.1 applies the flat-bet 1.15 figure to all play, which makes the band too narrow for spread play. Version 1.0.2 scales the band with your logged spread using the figures above. Thanks to Don Schlesinger for the correction.

How much to trust it

House-edge math is precise. Counting EV is an estimate — treat it as roughly ±30%. Real results depend on your exact bet ramp, count frequencies, and conditions, which only full simulation software can model. Use the EV numbers comparatively — 3:2 vs 6:5, hand-shuffle vs CSM, deep vs shallow penetration — rather than as a paycheck forecast. Your actual results over hundreds of hours are the real measurement.

Every actual dollar figure in the app is exact arithmetic on the sessions you logged — no estimation anywhere. $/hour is total P/L divided by total hours for whatever is being grouped.
